A plain statement of what a Bruin customer owns: pipelines as SQL, Python, and YAML in your own Git repository, data that never leaves your own warehouse, and an Apache 2.0 CLI that runs anywhere, including inside Airflow. It covers what Bruin Cloud adds, what happens if you stop paying, and the one place a dependency exists. Written to be checked, not believed.
