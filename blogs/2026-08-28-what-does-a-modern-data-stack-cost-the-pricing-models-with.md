A modern data stack is priced by four different meters: per monthly active row (Fivetran), per developer seat (dbt Cloud), per credit (Airbyte), and per environment-hour (Amazon MWAA), plus warehouse compute. This explainer shows what each meter charges at list price, which ones grow faster than your data, and why consolidating the stack into one open-source runtime is the largest single saving.
