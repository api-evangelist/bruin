Agentic data engineering is the practice of letting an AI coding agent build, run, test, and repair data pipelines, with a human reviewing the diff. This explainer covers what an agent can actually do for a pipeline, what the pipeline has to look like for that to be safe, how MCP connects the agent, and where Bruin, dbt's MCP server, Databricks Genie, and Dagster Compass fit.
