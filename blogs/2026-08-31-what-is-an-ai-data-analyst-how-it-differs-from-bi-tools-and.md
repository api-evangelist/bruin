An AI data analyst is a system that answers business questions from live, governed company data in plain English, inside the tools a team already uses, and can build dashboards and act on the answer. This explainer covers what it is, how it differs from a BI tool and from ChatGPT on a spreadsheet, what makes its answers accurate, and how to evaluate one.
