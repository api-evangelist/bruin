ELT means extract, load, transform: copy raw data into the warehouse first, then transform it there with SQL. ETL transforms before loading. This explainer covers the difference, why warehouses made ELT the default, when ETL still wins, and how Bruin, dbt, Fivetran, and Airbyte fit each pattern.
